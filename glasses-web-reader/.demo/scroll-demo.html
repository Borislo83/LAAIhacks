Three ways to scroll on the G2

The same article on three mock lenses. Press swipe down a few times and watch how each one moves. The glasses can't animate on their own; every change is the app sending new text over Bluetooth.

Arrow keys work too. Raise the delay to see how style C feels if the glasses update slowly.

A. Slides

before

One section per screen. Each swipe redraws the whole display (a short blank flash), and nothing from the last screen stays.

B. Big steps

earlier

One stream of text. Each swipe jumps 6 lines in one go; the bottom 3 lines move to the top, so you keep your place. No flash, but it is still an instant jump.

C. Quick steps

built now

The same 6-line move, rolled a line at a time and finished in about a quarter second. It starts fast and settles. When Bluetooth is too slow to show every line, it skips some so the swipe still ends on time. It reads as the text rolling up. The swipe takes longer to finish, and how smooth it looks depends on the real Bluetooth speed.

Not possible on the glasses: a true pixel-smooth slide like a phone. The display only shows whole lines in fixed boxes, so movement can only happen a line at a time.
