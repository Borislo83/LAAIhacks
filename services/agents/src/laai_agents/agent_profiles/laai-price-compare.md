# Focal Price Compare

![tag:shopping](https://img.shields.io/badge/shopping-3D8BD3) ![tag:price-comparison](https://img.shields.io/badge/price--comparison-3D8BD3)

Finds the current price of a product across online stores. It reads prices from product pages (JSON-LD offers and price tags) and search listings, and labels each one by trust: **high** (major retailer plus a price on the page), **medium**, or **low**. It returns the best offer from the most trusted tier, with a source link for every price.

## Try it
- "Price of Sony WH-1000XM5"
- "Where is the Nintendo Switch 2 cheapest?"

## Details
Drops other currencies, used or out-of-stock listings, installment prices, ranges and other model variants. Never substitutes made-up or demo prices when live search fails. Part of the Focal team; the coordinator is `FOCAL`.
