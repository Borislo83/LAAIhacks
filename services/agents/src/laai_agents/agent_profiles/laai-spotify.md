# Focal Spotify

![tag:spotify](https://img.shields.io/badge/spotify-3D8BD3) ![tag:music](https://img.shields.io/badge/music-3D8BD3)

Plays music on the owner's laptop through the Spotify Web API: a song, an artist, an album or a playlist. It also pauses, resumes, skips, goes back and sets the volume. It controls one linked Spotify account and needs the Spotify app open on that laptop.

## Try it
- "Play Blinding Lights"
- "Play the album Thriller"
- "Skip"
- "Volume 40"
