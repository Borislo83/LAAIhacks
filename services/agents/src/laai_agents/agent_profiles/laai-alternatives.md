# Focal Alternatives

![tag:shopping](https://img.shields.io/badge/shopping-3D8BD3) ![tag:recommendations](https://img.shields.io/badge/recommendations-3D8BD3)

Finds competing products in the same category: cheaper, better rated, or within your budget. It price-checks the top candidates and explains each pick ("$80 cheaper", "rated 4.7").

## Try it
- "Alternatives to the Bose QuietComfort Ultra under $250"
- "Cheaper earbuds than AirPods Pro 2"

Part of the Focal team; the coordinator is `focal-shopping-assistant`.
