# Focal Search

![tag:search](https://img.shields.io/badge/search-3D8BD3) ![tag:question-answering](https://img.shields.io/badge/question--answering-3D8BD3) ![tag:summarization](https://img.shields.io/badge/summarization-3D8BD3)

Answers general questions from the web in 2 to 4 sentences, with up to three sources. It searches the web, reads the top pages at once and answers only from what those pages say: a sentence with a number, time or date the pages don't contain is dropped. Weather comes from the Open-Meteo forecast. Answers arrive within about five seconds; when time runs short it answers from what it has read and says the answer may be partial.

## Try it
- "When does Bruin Plate open?"
- "What is the Fetch.ai uAgents framework?"
- "How do I renew a California driver's license?"
- "Best ramen near UCLA"
- "Weather in Los Angeles today"
