# Focal Amazon Buyer

![tag:shopping](https://img.shields.io/badge/shopping-3D8BD3) ![tag:amazon](https://img.shields.io/badge/amazon-3D8BD3)

Turns "buy this" into an Amazon cart addition. It finds candidate amazon.com listings, prepares a cart addition for the listing you pick, and adds it to the cart **only after you approve**.

**Cart only:** checkout, payment and order placement are disabled in code. Approvals are accepted only from the Focal coordinator, after the shopper approves on a review card in ASI:One.

## Try it
- "Find the Amazon listing for the Anker Soundcore Space Q45"

Part of the Focal team; the coordinator is `FOCAL`.
