The lens

One glance, one answer.

Look at a product page. Focal puts one line on your Even Realities G2: the best price it can source and what you'd save. Then it gets out of the way.

The architecture

Simple to wear, not simple inside.

The glasses hand the page to Focal's orchestrator. It works out what you need, checks what you've allowed, and keeps track of every approval.

The Agentverse

Three specialists, at the same time.

The Focal coordinator on Fetch.ai Agentverse sends the price, alternatives and reviews agents out in parallel. Each one fails on its own, so one slow site never blocks the answer.

The sources

No source, no number.

Every price comes back with where it was found and how far to trust it. If a number can't be backed up, Focal doesn't show it.

The approval gate

Nothing happens without your OK.

Ask Focal to add something to your Amazon cart and it shows you the exact listing first. You approve, it adds it and checks it's there. It has no way to check out. Messages to friends work the same way: you see the draft, you send it.

The Studio

A team that remembers you.

Tell Focal Studio what matters once: price, quality or balanced. Every agent works to it, and your choices carry across the glasses and the Studio.
