Privacy policy

Updated October 17, 2026

Focal LAAIHACKS is a hackathon prototype operated by the project team. This page explains the information used by its dashboard, orchestrator, agents, and glasses integration.

Account information

When you choose Google sign-in, Google provides basic identity information, including your email address and profile information. Supabase Authentication manages your account and session. Focal does not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail, Google Drive, or Calendar.

Information you save or submit

The service stores account-linked display and shopping preferences, your private notes, and submitted page or product context. Workflow storage includes sessions, pages, proposed actions, approvals, execution results, errors, and timestamps. Information submitted for research can include product names, page text, page URLs, and prices.

Your saved notes are stored with your account and are not sent to AI models or research agents. Do not enter passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information into notes or research prompts.

How information is used

Account information identifies you and controls access to your saved data. Preferences customize the display and research ordering. Page context and approved actions are used to carry out the requested research and show results. Workflow records support status reporting, retries, and troubleshooting.

Service providers

Supabase provides authentication and database storage. Depending on the configured research path, product queries and page context may be sent to ASI:One, search providers, and merchant or review websites. The research service uses Fetch.ai uAgents. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

These public information pages do not use advertising or analytics scripts. The hosting provider processes ordinary web requests. The authenticated dashboard may load fonts from Google Fonts and its Supabase SDK from jsDelivr.

Retention and your choices

You can change your saved preferences and notes in the dashboard and sign out at any time. Saved information remains until you request removal or project operators remove it. Session expiration does not itself guarantee immediate deletion of stored records. Deleting a Supabase account cascades related application data in the project's database.

You can revoke Focal's Google access through your Google Account settings. To request access, correction, account deletion, or removal of stored information, email borislo830@gmail.com.

Questions and updates

Contact borislo830@gmail.com with privacy questions. This prototype may change; material updates will be reflected on this page with a new update date.
